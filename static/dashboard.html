How it works

Three steps from "here's $1" to a safe purchase

The owner sets the rules once in plain English. The agent shops on its own. Anything risky stops before money moves.

1

Fund the tab with PayPal

The owner tops up once through PayPal Checkout (Orders v2). When the tab runs dry, the agent gets a 402 and asks its human for more.

  • Real sandbox captures
  • Unused credit refunds back to PayPal
2

The agent buys inside the rules

Deterministic layers run first: allowlist, per-vendor budgets, caps, unsafe URLs, purpose required. Then Gemini judges what rules can't express.

  • Prompt injection is blocked
  • If Gemini is down, a human decides
3

You approve, refund and watch

Purchases over $0.30 wait for your PayPal approval. Refund windows, renewals and webhooks show up on a Bryntum timeline that suggests refunds before deadlines close.

  • Signature-verified PayPal webhooks
  • Nothing is refunded without you
