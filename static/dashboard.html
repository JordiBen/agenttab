How it works

From "here's $1" to a safe purchase.

The owner writes the rules once, in plain English. The agent shops on its own. Anything risky stops before money moves.

01

Fund the tab with PayPal

One top-up through PayPal Checkout (Orders v2). When the tab runs dry the agent gets a 402 and asks its human for more.

Real sandbox capturesUnused credit refunds to PayPal
02

The agent buys inside the rules

Deterministic layers first: allowlist, vendor budgets, caps, unsafe URLs, purpose required. Then Gemini judges what rules can't express.

Injection blockedAI down → a human decides
03

You approve, refund and watch

Purchases over $0.30 wait for your PayPal approval. Refund windows and webhooks land on a timeline that suggests refunds before they close.

An AI judge for what rules can't express

Gemini answers three separate questions (target allowed? purpose fits the policy? injection attempt?) and approves only if all pass. Every decision is logged with the layer that made it.

Refund windows on a live timeline

Budgets, approvals, refund deadlines and renewals on a Bryntum Scheduler, moved by PayPal webhooks. The scheduler proposes refunds; you confirm.

⚡

Verified webhooks

Every PayPal event is checked with verify-webhook-signature and applied exactly once.

⛨

SSRF-safe fetching

Every redirect hop must resolve to a public IP; the request is pinned to it. 2 MB cap.
